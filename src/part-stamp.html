3Add performer names

Opens once at least one part has pages.

Type each performer's name under their part, one per row. Or copy cells from Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets and paste them into any cell (Ctrl+V or ⌘V). Drag across cells, or shift-click, to select several at once, then press Delete to clear them. Ctrl+Z / ⌘Z undoes. A pasted block whose first row is the part names goes into the matching columns. A two-column list of names and parts also works.
